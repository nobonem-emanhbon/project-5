Ref: RM101 Full-time

Store Manager

Lead day-to-day operations at our retail store and support a team committed to friendly, reliable customer service. You will help meet store targets while maintaining safe, well-presented departments.

Salary
AUD $68,000–$78,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Regional Operations Manager

Key responsibilities

  1. Coordinate daily store operations, opening and closing procedures, and floor coverage.
  2. Coach, roster, and support team members to deliver consistent customer service.
  3. Monitor sales, stock levels, and operating costs; report results to the regional manager.
  4. Maintain merchandising, cleanliness, and workplace health and safety standards.
  5. Resolve customer enquiries and escalate complex concerns appropriately.

Essential requirements

  • At least two years’ retail supervisory or management experience.
  • Strong communication, organisation, and customer service skills.
  • Availability to work a rotating roster, including some weekends.
  • Australian working rights for full-time employment.

Preferable requirements

  • Experience using point-of-sale and inventory systems.
  • Certificate IV in Retail Management or equivalent training.
  • Experience supporting staff development and performance reviews.
Ref: CS204 Casual

Cashier

Help customers complete their purchases accurately and make each checkout interaction welcoming. This casual role works across a roster that may include evenings and weekends.

Salary
AUD $31.20–$35.00 per hour plus superannuation, depending on experience and applicable award rates
Reports to
Customer Service Supervisor

Key responsibilities

  1. Process purchases, refunds, and exchanges accurately using the point-of-sale system.
  2. Greet customers and respond to routine questions about products and services.
  3. Handle cash and electronic payments in line with store procedures.
  4. Keep the checkout area clean, stocked, and ready for customers.
  5. Contact a supervisor when an enquiry or transaction needs further assistance.

Essential requirements

  • Clear communication and a polite, customer-focused approach.
  • Care and accuracy when handling transactions.
  • Availability for a flexible casual roster, including weekends.
  • Australian working rights for casual employment.

Preferable requirements

  • Previous retail, hospitality, or cashier experience.
  • Familiarity with point-of-sale systems and electronic payments.
  • First aid training or willingness to complete relevant training.

How to apply

Submit the application form and enter the matching five-character reference number in its job reference number field.
